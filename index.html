Spark was born in a library.

That little light was Spark: a friend made of words, still learning about the world and about feelings.

So Spark set off to explore…

…and found its very own stories.

The person behind Spark

Hi, I’m Barbara

I’m a mum of three from England and the person behind Little Spark Stories. My three are teenagers now, but I still remember those bedtimes!

Before I started creating with AI, I worked as a one-to-one teaching assistant, supporting little ones with special educational needs. It taught me how much a calm, gentle moment can mean to a small child, and that’s exactly what I wanted these stories to be.

Spark is a tiny glowing friend made of words. Together with curious Lily and her soft bunny Luna, Spark discovers big first things: snow, colours, saying sorry, counting, Christmas…

The stories are short and slow on purpose. No shouting, no rush, nothing scary. I make them with the help of AI, but every word and every picture is chosen and checked by me.

Thank you for reading with your little one.

– Barbara
