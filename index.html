Hi, I'm Barbara

I'm a mum from England and the person behind Little Spark Stories. Before I started creating with AI, I worked as a teaching assistant, supporting little ones one-to-one. It taught me how much a calm, gentle moment can mean to a small child, and that's exactly what I wanted these stories to be.

Spark is a tiny glowing friend made of words. Together with curious Lily and her soft bunny Luna, Spark discovers big first things: snow, colours, saying sorry, counting, Christmas...

The stories are short and slow on purpose. No shouting, no rush, nothing scary. I make them with the help of AI, but every word and every picture is chosen and checked by me.

Thank you for reading with your little one.

– Barbara

For parents

Made for ages 2–5, with gentle British narration, soft colours and just one feeling at a time, so there's time to notice it and name it together. Spark is an AI too – and that's part of the story.
