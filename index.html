For parents

Little Spark Stories are made to be calm and low-stimulation: gentle voices, soft colours, slow pacing and no loud surprises. Each story explores just one feeling at a time, so little ones can understand it and feel safe. We are open about how we make them: the stories are created with the help of AI – Spark is an AI too, and that is part of the story – and every one is written, checked and approved by a human before it reaches your family.
